    Summary

    Veritas Academy needed a proper website for marketing and advertising that would also give its students easy access to online exams and the question bank archive. I was given ownership of this project. It was a large-scale project that took several months to complete.

    Features

    • Information pages about the services Veritas Academy provides
    • Information pages about Veritas Academy and its accomplishments
    • Marketing pages
    • Portals for students, teachers and staff
    • Student Portal features: lesson tracking, access to solvable digital AP, IB, SAT and IMAT exams and the digital question bank archive, exam score tracking
    • Teacher Portal features: lesson tracking, tracking student progress on digital exams, creating reports and accessing the question bank archive
    • Staff Portal features: role-based management of website page content and management of Veritas Academy's human resources

    Development Process

    On the Front-End part, all pages are built as .cshtml views with classic HTML, CSS and JavaScript. At first, I developed all the pages on demand with an SEO-friendly, simple Bootstrap structure without a theme. I was not satisfied with how the pages looked, so I proposed polishing and redesigning them. I then worked with the marketing manager on page content and closely with designer Utkan Kanberoglu on design decisions and building a theme. Using the design assets he provided, I created a CSS theme and redeveloped the pages according to the team's decisions.

    On the Back-End part, I initially used Entity Framework for data access. Later, because of the limited resources of the hosting environment, EF performance was not sufficient and the project required MS SQL stored procedures and ADO.NET. So I designed and built a custom data access layer, similar to this project in my GitHub repositories, to meet the project's performance requirements. I then developed the other software components (services, APIs, controllers) around it.

    Update Entry

    Entry Date : 31.05.2024

    I rebuilt this website with ASP.NET Core using Clean Architecture. During this process I added new features such as a Minimal API layer, detailed use cases, new services designed around the separation of concerns principle and data caching with MemoryCache, to fulfill the requirements of Clean Architecture.
