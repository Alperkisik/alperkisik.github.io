    About the Game

    During my first internship at Makronet Information Technologies, the company's lead developer asked me to build a simple card game. In the requested game, each card had two attributes, health and attack, like in Hearthstone. Each turn, a card attacks and the attacked card's health is reduced by the attacker's attack value. I found that concept a bit too simple, so I decided to take it to the next level and build a Warhammer Fantasy card game.

    Each card has a detailed set of stats based on the game Total War: Warhammer. In every combat, hit and block chances are calculated with formulas based on Total War: Warhammer. Each faction has a unique ability that I designed. To win, you must send all of your opponent's cards to the graveyard.

    How to Play

    On the first turn, the player chooses a card to place on the field, then the AI does the same. Each turn, the player chooses one of the following actions:

    • Attack: The player's card attacks the card on the opponent's side of the field. Depending on the result, the attack may hit or miss. If the attack hits, the attacked card loses HP according to the formula. If its HP drops to zero or below, the card goes to the graveyard.
    • Defend: Boosts the defense values of the player's card and ends the player's turn.
    • Power: If the chosen card has an ability, the player may use it.
    • Pick: The player puts another card from the deck on the field. The previous card returns to the deck with its current health.
    After the player picks an action, the matching event occurs and the turn passes to the AI. The AI chooses one of the same actions, then the turn passes back to the player. This continues until either the player or the AI runs out of cards.

    Features

    • 3 playable Warhammer Fantasy factions
    • Each faction has a unique ability and unit roster
    • Randomly generated battle decks
    • Turn-based card combat
